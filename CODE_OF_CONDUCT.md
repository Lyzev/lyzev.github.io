# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and considerate. Harassment, insults, trolling, and publishing others' private information are not tolerated in any project space, including the [Discord](https://lyzev.dev/discord) server.

Report violations via [Discord](https://lyzev.dev/discord) or to [contact@lyzev.dev](mailto:contact@lyzev.dev). Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. Violations may lead to a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban, depending on severity.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
